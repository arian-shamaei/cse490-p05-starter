# The lecture's pelican prompt

The sentence that built the still pelican in lecture, for your starting point:

> Build a pelican riding a bicycle in Blender, from primitives: a body, a head with a
> long beak and pouch, two wings, two legs on the pedals, a bicycle with a frame, two
> wheels, handlebars and a seat. Give it simple flat materials, a sun lamp, and a camera
> that frames the whole thing.

Ask for it in your own words. The ride is what you add.
